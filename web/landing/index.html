Four traditions, one table

Mahjong has traveled the world for a century, gathering meaning at every table. Maharajong brings it home to India — braiding four great game traditions into one set, dressed in the art of rangoli and the depth of Vedic symbol.

中

Chinese

The strategic heart — melds, pungs and kongs, and the flexible pursuit of a winning hand.

日

Japanese

Riichi discipline — the "Ready" declaration and the quiet art of defensive play.

★

American

The social Charleston pass and evolving hands that keep every player in the game.

॥

Indian

Rummy-style runs, festival scoring, and the warmth of an Indian card night.

At the Table

How it plays

Familiar if you've played mahjong, welcoming if you haven't — four short beats to your first win.

1

The Charleston

Warm up by passing three tiles right, across and left — the Charleston, borrowed from American mahjong and the passing games of an Indian card night.

2

Draw & build

Draw or claim a discard to form melds — pungs, kongs, and Rummy-style runs. Chakra wilds bend the rules for creative sets.

3

Declare Ready

One tile from winning with a concealed hand? Declare Ready for bonus points — and play defense as the wall runs down.

4

Call "Maharajah!"

Complete four melds and a pair — or a special festival hand — and declare "Maharajah!" to win the round in style.
